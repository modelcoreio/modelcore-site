From sample to production.

Most teams test fit on a sample, run a pilot, then scale. Nothing is committed until the data has passed your own evaluation.

  1. Brief

    Tell us the model, stage, and constraints. We reply with the relevant catalogue and options.

  2. Sample

    A representative slice and the full datasheet, free, to run through your evaluation.

  3. Pilot

    A scoped delivery against acceptance criteria you set, before any large commitment.

  4. Production

    Full delivery to your storage, on one schedule or in rolling batches.

  5. Refresh

    New data, new languages, or new labels as your training needs change.

What every datasheet covers.

The documentation your research and legal teams will ask for, delivered with every sample and every order.

  • Source and collection methodWhere the data came from and how it was gathered.
  • Rights and consentThe license chain and consent basis, with an audit trail.
  • CompositionLanguages, domains, demographics, and how they are balanced.
  • AnnotationThe label schema, guidelines, and who applied them.
  • Quality resultsHow quality was measured on this data, and what was found.
  • Cleaning and deduplicationFiltering, near-duplicate removal, and contamination checks.
  • Known limitationsGaps, biases, and uses the data is not suited for.
  • VersioningWhat changed between deliveries, so results stay reproducible.
