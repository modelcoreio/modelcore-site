Business operations data for enterprise AI.

Real records of how companies work, from messaging and documents to CRM, tickets, and code. Licensed directly from the companies that produced them, and anonymized before delivery.

What we can supply

  • Workplace messaging and email
  • Documents, wikis, and SOPs
  • CRM records and deal cycles
  • Support tickets and call notes
  • Private code, issues, and incident logs
  • Project trackers
  • Finance, HR, and procurement workflows

Labels and enrichment

  • Anonymized people and companies
  • Thread and workflow structure
  • Roles
  • Outcomes, such as resolution or deal stage
  • Source-system metadata

Formats and delivery

JSONL organized by company and source system, with schemas documented. Delivered to AWS S3, Google Cloud Storage, Azure, or your own storage, with a datasheet and provenance records.

Common uses

  • Enterprise agents
  • Workflow automation
  • Computer and tool use
  • Sales and support assistants
  • Agent evaluation on realistic tasks

Questions

Where does this data come from?

Directly from companies, licensed with their sign-off and scoped system by system.

How is it anonymized?

People, companies, and confidential identifiers are masked before delivery, and no buyer receives raw content.
