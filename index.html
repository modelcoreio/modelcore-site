Common questions.

What do I get when I license data?

The source data, whether audio, video, images, text, code, sensor logs, or anonymized business records, cleaned, deduplicated, and consistently formatted. Plus enrichment as JSON and JSONL sidecars: transcripts, captions, boxes, entities, test results, or workflow labels depending on the data type. Every delivery includes a datasheet and the documentation behind the rights.

Can I see a sample before committing?

Yes. Request the catalogue or describe what you need, and we send a representative sample and datasheet so you can evaluate fit before any licensing conversation.

What does rights-cleared mean?

Each record carries documented consent and a license chain we can show you. You receive an audit trail tying the data to its source and rights basis, so your legal team can defend its use.

What if the data I need isn't in the catalogue?

We collect it. Share a spec covering language, domain, modality, annotation schema, and volume, and our sourcing network and enrichment pipeline build it to order.

How is data delivered?

As JSON and JSONL alongside the raw media, with WebDataset or Parquet on request. We deliver to AWS S3, Google Cloud Storage, Azure, or your own bucket.

Do you offer exclusive licenses?

Many datasets are available both non-exclusively and exclusively. Exclusive and first-look deals are scoped case by case.

I own content or company data. How do I get paid for it?

Tell us what you hold, whether that is a media library or your company's operational systems. We review the rights you control, enrich the content into a licensable product, and license it to vetted AI buyers on terms you set. You keep ownership and earn recurring revenue.

How do you handle privacy and personal data?

Collection follows documented consent, and we screen and redact personal information appropriately for each modality. Details are in each dataset's datasheet and our Privacy Policy and Data Processing Addendum.

Tell us what you need.

Data to train on, or data to license. We reply within one business day.
