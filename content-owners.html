Questions from content owners.

Do we keep ownership of our content?

Yes. You license content on defined terms; you never sell it outright. You choose exclusive or non-exclusive deals and define exclusions up front.

What kinds of content do AI labs license?

Film and TV, music and audio, podcasts, news, publishing, photography, archives, and user-generated video. Quality, diversity, and clean rights matter more than volume alone.

What does it cost us?

Nothing up front. We handle the audit, enrichment, and packaging, and you earn a transparent revenue share on what is licensed.

How do we know where our content goes?

Every license is with a vetted buyer on defined terms, and you get a full record of where your content went and how it is used.
