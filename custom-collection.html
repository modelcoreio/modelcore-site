How a project runs.

  1. Scope

    We agree the spec, schema, volume, quality bar, and rights model with your team.

  2. Source

    We recruit contributors or license content, with documented consent and rights.

  3. Collect

    Managed collection with quality gates and demographic balancing.

  4. Enrich

    Transcription, labeling, preferences, and validation by trained or expert annotators.

  5. Deliver

    Model-ready output in your format, with a datasheet and audit trail.
