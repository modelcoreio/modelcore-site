The details, up front.

Why do AI labs want our data?

Frontier models are being trained to work inside companies, using real tools, workflows, and decisions. Public and synthetic data can't replicate that. Your operational data captures how organizations actually run.

How is compensation calculated?

On data volume, the systems you include, and the depth and uniqueness of the data. We give you an estimate before anything moves.

Is there any cost to us?

No. We cover extraction, anonymization, and transfer. There is no upfront cost, setup fee, or deduction.

Who sees our raw data?

Extraction and anonymization are automated. Sensitive identifiers are removed before any buyer sees the data, and no buyer or third party receives your raw content. All access is logged.

Do we keep ownership?

Yes. You license data on defined terms, you can set exclusions, and raw data is never resold.

When do we get paid?

By wire once the anonymized dataset is delivered, typically within a few weeks of a signed agreement depending on scope.
