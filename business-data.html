How it works.

  1. Estimate

    We review the systems you use and the data you'd consider, and estimate what it's worth. No access needed.

  2. Agree scope

    You choose the systems, date ranges, and exclusions. It's all written into an agreement before any data moves.

  3. Export and de-identify

    Your team exports the agreed data. A de-identification specialist named in your agreement removes names, contact details, and confidential identifiers.

  4. Approve and get paid

    You review a sample of the final dataset. Once you approve, it's licensed to a vetted buyer and you're paid under your agreement.

Nothing moves without your sign-off.

The details, up front.

Why do AI labs want our data?

Frontier models are being trained to work inside companies, using real tools, workflows, and decisions. Public and synthetic data can't replicate that. Your operational data captures how organizations actually run.

How is compensation calculated?

On data volume, the systems you include, and the depth and uniqueness of the data. We give you an estimate before anything moves.

Is there any cost to us?

No. There is no upfront cost or setup fee. ModelCore is paid out of the licensing deal, on terms set out in your agreement.

Do we need to give you access to our systems?

No. Your team exports the agreed data using your platforms' own admin tools, so you never share logins. During scoping we confirm what each platform's terms allow.

Who sees our raw data?

Only your team and the party that de-identifies it, which is named in your agreement and bound by contract. Buyers only ever receive the de-identified dataset you approved.

Do we keep ownership?

Yes. You license data on defined terms, you can set exclusions, and raw data is never resold.

When do we get paid?

Under the terms of your agreement, once the approved dataset is delivered to the buyer.
